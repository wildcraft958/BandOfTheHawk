6. EXCLUDED FROM THE WRITE-UP
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Zero-shot recall on the held-out verticals is NOT reported. The holdout is
  contaminated: SIM_SWAP is both a held-out scripted vertical and a legal
  action in the learned attacker's space, and the attacker uses it. The
  defender therefore trains on sim_swap traffic and is then asked whether it
  generalises to sim_swap. The number is uninterpretable in both directions
  and is omitted rather than explained away.

  A second limitation, stated rather than hidden: during the live phase the
  defender trains only on what the learned attacker produces -- one attack
  shape per round, a few hundred rows. The scripted verticals do not run.
  A detector fitted on that is narrower than a deployed one would be.
